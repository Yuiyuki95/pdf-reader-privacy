PDF Reader

Effective date: 20 October 2026. This policy describes the PDF Reader Android app (com.ken.pdfreader). PDF_Reader / Yuki_K.

Documents and signatures

The app reads PDFs and signature images that you choose through Android’s file picker. It stores private copies of imported PDFs, edited PDFs, reading position, bookmarks, language and reading preferences, and your latest saved signature on your device. Editing and signing create new PDF copies. The app does not upload these files or send document contents, filenames, signatures or reading history to the developer.

Local processing

Reading, thumbnails, page organisation, merging, splitting, extracting, deleting pages and visible image signatures run on your device. The app has no Internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics service or account registration. PDFBox-Android and Bouncy Castle are bundled for local PDF processing; their notices are available in the app.

Word conversion

The app also reads .docx files that you choose and converts them to PDF locally. The original Word file is not modified. Temporary Word and PDF copies are held in app-private cache during conversion and deleted when the operation ends; interrupted work is cleaned up later. The resulting PDF is stored in your local library. No conversion service or network upload is used.

Your sharing and printing choices

If you choose Share, the PDF you select is handed to the app or service you choose using Android’s sharing controls. Printing hands that PDF, or prepared page output, to Android’s print service. Saving a copy writes to the destination you choose. A selected file provider, share destination or printer service may use networks and process data under its own policy. The developer does not automatically receive those documents.

Purchases and support

If distributed as a paid download, Google Play processes the purchase under its own policies. PDF Reader does not request or store payment card details. If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives the information you choose to include and uses it to respond to your enquiry. Do not send private documents or signatures unless you intend to share them for support.

Retention and deletion

Private document copies and reading records remain until removed in the app, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. You can remove your saved signature from the signature menu. Temporary processing files are used locally; interrupted work may leave temporary cache data until cleanup or Android clears the cache. Copies you exported or shared are outside the app and must be deleted at their destinations.

Device protection

App-private storage and Android access controls protect local app data; the app does not promise separate file encryption. Backup of app data is disabled in the app manifest. Device compromise, exported copies and the services you choose can affect confidentiality.

Children and changes

This is a general document utility and does not include child accounts, social features or behavioural advertising. No personal-data collection is intentionally directed at children. The developer may update this policy when app behaviour changes; the effective date identifies the current policy.

Support data and this website

Support emails and attachments are retained only as needed to resolve your enquiry and meet legal obligations. You may request their deletion at yukigp31@gmail.com; legally required records may be retained. This policy website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process visitor IP addresses and technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, use the developer’s public contact email under App support on the PDF Reader Google Play listing. The online version of this policy includes that contact address.
